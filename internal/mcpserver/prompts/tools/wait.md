Block until an unread message newer than since_id arrives for an address (default: your signed-on identity and its subagents; anonymous callers must pass address), then return its header(s) like list; or return timed_out=true. Use instead of polling list. timeout_seconds defaults to 60 and is capped at 300; keep it below your MCP client's tool-call timeout. Progress notifications are sent every 15 seconds when the client supplies a progress token. Pass since_id (the newest id you have seen) to ignore older unread mail.
